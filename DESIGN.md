# Design

## Theme
Dark, premium, editorial. High-contrast typography on deep dark backgrounds.

## Colors
- **Background**: `#0A0A0F` (Deep dark)
- **Text**: `#F0EBE3` (Warm off-white)
- **Primary**: `#00A19B` (Teal)
- **Accent**: `#7B61FF` (Purple)
- **Danger**: `#FF4560` (Red)

## Typography
- **Headings**: Serif italic uppercase (editorial feel)
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (Inter)
- **Data**: Monospace (DM Mono) for addresses and numeric values

## Layout
- **Rhythm**: No nested cards. Consistent padding and breathing room.
- **Hierarchy**: Contrast through scale and weight contrast (≥1.25 ratio).

## Motion
- **Entrance**: Purposeful animations on load.
- **State**: Smooth transitions for the kill switch and policy updates.
- **Easing**: Exponential curves (no bounce).
